For Dr. Natalie — session notes
Bringing this schedule to therapy as a working draft, not a finished plan.
Theme
Structure without rigidity
7 hrs/day Mon–Thu. Fri–Sun completely unscheduled. The goal is rhythm, not performance.
Values
Each block ties to something I care about
Guitar, ASL, reading, project — these aren't productivity. They're identity-building during a transition period.
Volunteer
VoteRiders + Cornerstones (both verified real)
Structured, community-facing, lower-chaos. Fits burnout recovery. Uses existing bureaucracy + crisis-navigation skills.
Project
App or cookbook — one per day, not both
Two passion projects is fine. Trying to do both on the same day leads to paralysis. Rule: pick one per session.
Gaming
2 hrs max, not daily — built in, not hidden
Mon and Wed optional windows. Named openly in the schedule so it doesn't feel like a secret or a failure.
Tensions
Things to talk through with Dr. Natalie
1) How rigid vs flexible should this be on hard days? 2) What does "failure" look like and how do I recover without shame? 3) Is volunteer work the right re-entry pace right now?
