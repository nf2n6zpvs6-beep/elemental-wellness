The person behind the app

Built from experience,
given freely.

I built this because I've been through my own mental health battles and wanted something simple I could use to check in with myself in the moment and actually get a response that feels helpful and personal. So I made the idea a reality with a little help from Claude, and honestly, I'm so stoked to be creating with AI.

I hold a Master's in Psychology, and while I'm not a licensed clinician, I understand the weight of what people carry. This app offers grounding exercises, affirmations, journal prompts, breathing techniques, motivational messages, and tarot questions, all personalized to your element and how you're feeling right now.

No paywalls. No subscriptions. Just support, whenever you need it.

Because everyone deserves access to tools that help them feel grounded, clear, and capable, regardless of what's in their bank account.

Phynadi

⚠️ Elemental Wellness is a wellness support tool, not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. If you are in crisis, please reach out to a licensed professional or call/text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
